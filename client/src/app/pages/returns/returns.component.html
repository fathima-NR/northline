Returns

30 days to change your mind.

How a return works

Email hello@northline.store with your order date and the product name. We send a prepaid label for standard-size items. Pack the product in its original box with tags and accessories. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after we inspect the return.

What we accept

Unused items in resalable condition. Headphones and watches must include the case and paperwork. Opened earbuds can be returned if the hygiene seal is intact. Final-sale items are marked on the product page when they apply.

Damaged on arrival

Photograph the parcel and the product, then write to us within 7 days. We replace it or refund the order, including shipping.
