Shipping

Standard delivery on every in-stock order.

Standard shipping

Orders placed before 2:00 pm on a business day leave our warehouse the same day. Delivery takes 3–5 business days inside the continental United States. Shipping is $10, and free when the merchandise total is $50 or more.

What you receive

You get an order confirmation as soon as checkout finishes, and the order appears under Your orders with the status Processing. When it leaves the building the status changes to Out for delivery.

Address

We ship to street addresses and apartments. Add a gate code or delivery note at checkout if the carrier needs it. We do not ship to P.O. boxes for cameras or watches.
